**Analytics → Usage** answers "how much was sent, and what became of it" for a stretch of
time you choose. It shows **counts only**: messages sent, delivered, failed and pending,
and SMS parts. It never shows a recipient's number, a message's text, or money.

## Who sees what

| You are | You see |
| :--- | :--- |
| A member of an account, with permission to see analytics reports | Your own account's usage. |
| A reseller | **My customers** — every customer you look after, added up and one by one — or **My account**. |
| FireFlo's platform staff | **All customers** — every account and reseller on the platform — or one of them. |

A reseller never sees another reseller's customers, and a customer never sees its
reseller's figures.

## The chart

Choose the period at the top:

| Period | One bar is |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1 hr** | 5 minutes |
| **24 hr** | 1 hour |
| **1 week** | 1 day |
| **1 month** | 1 day |

- **Earlier** and **Later** move one whole period back or forward; **Now** returns to the
  latest.
- **All channels** narrows the chart and everything under it to one channel.
- **Sent / Received** switches between the messages you sent and the ones that came in.
- **Bars / Lines** draws the same figures as stacked bars (delivered, pending, failed) or
  as lines.

The **1 hr** view brings itself up to date every minute. A message still waiting for its
delivery report counts as **Pending**, and moves to Delivered or Failed when the report
arrives — so the newest bars settle over the following minutes.

<Note>
Five-minute figures are kept for 48 hours and hourly figures for 100 days, so **Earlier**
stops there for **1 hr** and **24 hr**. Daily figures are kept for good.
</Note>

## The totals

Under the chart: **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Failed**, **Pending** and **Parts** for the
period, with Sent compared against the period before. Resellers and staff also see how
many customers had any traffic.

## The tables

**By customer** (resellers and staff) lists every customer with traffic in the period:
sent, delivered, delivery rate, failed, pending, parts and their share of the total. Sort
by any column, search by name, and choose how many rows a page holds.

Choose a customer's name to open its figures **stretch by stretch** — one row per five
minutes, hour or day, newest first. **Older** and **Newer** page through its history. On
your own account's Usage this table is always shown.

**Export CSV** on either table downloads it as a file.

## How it stays quick

Usage does not count messages when you open it. OMNI counts them in the background, per
five minutes, per hour and per day, and the page adds up those counts — so it opens as
quickly with millions of messages as with a few hundred.

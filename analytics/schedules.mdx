A schedule sends a report on its own, covering the period just ended: yesterday's traffic
every morning, last week's delivery every Monday, last month's spend on the 1st. Each run
also keeps its file in [Downloads](/using/downloads).

## Setting one up

Open a report and choose **Schedule**, or **Analytics → Schedules → New schedule**.
Scheduling a built-in report saves a copy of it, shared with your team, and schedules the
copy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="How often">
    Every day, every week (on a day you choose) or every month (on a day from the 1st to
    the 28th), at an hour — in your account's time zone. A report goes out within a few
    minutes of that hour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Covering">
    The period each report covers: yesterday, today so far, last week, the last 7 days,
    this month so far, last month, or the last 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="As">
    A **CSV file**, an **Excel file**, or a **summary in the email**: each total, with its
    change when the report compares, and the first 25 rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to">
    **Email** — up to 20 addresses — and, if your account has the OMNI API, **Webhooks**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An account can have up to 50 schedules.

## By email

The email comes from the same sender as the panel's other emails, with the report's name
and period in the subject. A CSV or Excel file is attached when it is 10 MB or less; a larger one stays in
Downloads, and the email links there.

## To webhooks

With the OMNI API in your plan, a schedule can send the report to your own systems as a
signed `report.delivered` event — retried and logged like every other webhook. At least
one of your [webhook endpoints](/developers/webhooks) must listen for
`report.delivered`; until one does, the schedule can't be set to send there.

The event carries the report's columns, totals and up to 1,000 rows, and the address of
its file for the rest. See [Reports through the API](/developers/reports).

## Keeping an eye on them

The **Schedules** page lists each schedule with where it goes and how its last run went.

- **On** switches a schedule off and on.
- **Send one now** sends it at once, outside its schedule.
- **Recent runs** shows the last twenty: when, the period covered, and why one failed.
- **Change** and **Delete** do what they say.

A schedule sends as the person who set it up, with what they may see. If they leave the
team, its runs fail until someone else sets it up again. **After five failures in a row a
schedule is switched off**, and the person who set it up is emailed why. Fix the cause and
switch it back on.

## Who may

Setting up, changing and sending schedules needs **Schedule reports and choose where they
are sent** — given to the Owner and Admin roles by default. See
[Analytics](/analytics/reports#who-may).

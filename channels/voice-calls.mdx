Every call Voice hears of is kept in one **call log**, with its recording and what was
said when there is one, and filed against the contact. From OMNI you can also place
calls four ways: **click-to-call** from your own phone, **ringing someone into an IVR**,
a **voice broadcast**, and a **voice agent**.

## The call log

**Voice → Calls** lists every call: the ones that rang in, and the ones placed from OMNI
or from your phones. Each row shows when, who (the contact, or the number), how it was
made, the outcome, the length, and whether it was **Recorded**.

<Frame caption="Voice → Calls: every call, with its outcome and recording.">
  <img src="/images/channels/voice-calls.png" alt="The Calls list with incoming and outgoing calls" />
</Frame>

**How** a call was made:

| Kind | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Incoming** | It rang in on one of your numbers. |
| **From a phone** | Made from one of your organisation's phones. |
| **Click-to-call** | Placed from OMNI: your phone rang first, then theirs. |
| **Rung into an IVR** | OMNI rang them into an IVR. |
| **Broadcast** | Part of a voice broadcast. |
| **Voice agent** | A voice agent made it. |

Search by number or name, and filter by dates, direction (**Incoming** or **Outgoing**)
and outcome. A **missed** call is one that rang in and nobody took.

**Export** keeps the calls, as filtered, as a spreadsheet under
[Downloads](/using/downloads). On FireFlo's Voice, the export has no prices.

### Connect the full call log

Calls placed from OMNI are always logged. To also log calls that ring in and calls made
from your phones, the full call log must be connected — the Calls page asks for it until
it is:

- on **your own Voice**, someone who can configure channels connects it from
  **Voice → Calls**. Voice then reports calls as they happen, and OMNI also reads the
  call log every 10 minutes for anything missed;
- on **FireFlo's Voice**, FireFlo connects it — ask support@fireflo.au. You then see
  the calls on the numbers FireFlo gave your account.

### A call's page

Open a call to see its recording and its facts: the direction, your number it rang on,
the number it showed as, who took it or placed it, its length, why it ended and — on your
own Voice — what it was charged.

- **Recordings** play in OMNI, and you can skip through them.
- **What was said** shows the conversation's summary, its flags and each turn, when
  Voice has them.

### On the contact

Each contact's timeline shows their calls, with a **Calls** filter and **Listen** to play
a recording in place. The contact page counts their calls and missed calls. A caller who
isn't a contact yet is kept under their number and joined to the contact once there is
one.

## Click-to-call: your phone

**Call** on a contact's page, or **Place a call** on Voice → Calls, rings **your phone**
first; when you pick up, Voice dials the person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your phone">
    Under **Your phone**, pick the extension that rings for you and choose **Use this
    phone**. On FireFlo's Voice, FireFlo sets your extension — ask support@fireflo.au.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call">
    Choose **Call** (or enter a **Number** on Place a call). Your phone rings; pick up and
    Voice dials them. **Follow the call** opens its page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If it can't ring, OMNI says why — for example, your phone isn't online, or Voice is
busy. The call goes into the call log and the contact's timeline with who placed it.

You can call anyone except a contact who asked never to be contacted. A STOP to messages
doesn't stop you calling them.

## Ring into an IVR

**Ring into IVR** on a contact's page calls them and plays them one of your published
IVRs. See [Ring someone into an IVR](/channels/voice-numbers-and-ivrs#ring-someone-into-an-ivr).

## Voice broadcasts

<Note>
  Voice broadcasts need **your own Voice organisation**, which is billed for the calls.
</Note>

A voice broadcast rings everyone in a [broadcast](/using/broadcasts)'s audience into one
of your IVRs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make an IVR that runs on calls you place">
    In [Voice → IVRs](/channels/voice-numbers-and-ivrs#make-and-publish-an-ivr), make one
    with **Runs on calls you place**, design it and publish it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a broadcast on Voice">
    Choose the IVR. For each `{{tag}}` it says, give a column of your list or your own
    text — **What the IVR says for each person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the number and the hours">
    **Calls show** one of your numbers, or your caller ID. **Calling hours** and the
    **days** calls may be made default to Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 20:00, in the
    account's time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    People who opted out or are marked do-not-contact are left out. Voice rings the
    rest within the calling hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While it runs, **Pause**, **Resume** and **Cancel** reach Voice straight away — even
after everyone has been handed over and Voice is still ringing. The broadcast's
**Calls** card shows how many were **Answered**, the **Answer rate** and how many are
**Still to ring**; its header reads **Reached** and **Still to ring**.

| Outcome | Shows as |
| :--- | :--- |
| Reached | Delivered |
| Not reached, bad number, do-not-call or failed | Failed, with the reason |
| Never rung by the time it ends | Failed, "not called" |

Each person's broadcast call is on their timeline.

## Voice agents

<Note>
  Voice agents need **your own Voice organisation**, which is billed for their calls,
  and Voice's AI module. If the module is off, OMNI says so plainly.
</Note>

Voice agents are made in Voice (**Virtual agents**). **Voice → Voice agents** lists
them — "No agent uses it yet" marks one that isn't ready — and the latest calls they made
for your account, each with its summary.

### Call a contact with a voice agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    On their page, choose **Call with a voice agent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an agent">
    OMNI shows the line it opens with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it what it needs">
    Under **What the agent knows**, `{{name}}` is the contact's name. Add anything else
    its script uses, as a name and a value — `appointment` → `Tuesday at 3pm`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call">
    The agent rings them. **Follow the call** opens its page, where **What was said**
    shows the summary, the flags and each turn as Voice reports them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Calls with a voice agent are screened for opt-outs, do-not-contact and quiet hours.

## From your own software

The OMNI API lists calls and [places them](/developers/calls) — ringing someone into an
IVR or having a voice agent call them — with the same screening.

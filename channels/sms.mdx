SMS sends text messages from your **sender IDs**, using your approved **templates**
where your provider requires them. Replies land in the [Inbox](/using/inbox), every
message is on the contact's timeline, and delivery reports come back on their own.

**The plan must include SMS.** The menu then shows an **SMS** section with Dashboard,
Send SMS, Sender IDs, Templates and Settings.

## Set up SMS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how you connect">
    Open **SMS → Settings**. Under **Connection**, choose **FireFlo gateway** (included,
    nothing to set up) or **My own gateway**, and for your own enter its address, login
    and password. **Save and test** checks the login straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your default country">
    Under **Numbers**, the **Default country** is used for numbers typed without a
    country code — `IN` for India.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on DLT if you send to India">
    Under **DLT (India)**, switch on **We send to India under DLT** and enter your **DLT
    entity ID**. See [Sender IDs and DLT](/channels/sms-sender-ids-and-dlt).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a sender ID and add templates">
    Under **Sender IDs** and **Templates**, request the names and wording you will send.
    Your provider approves each one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On your own gateway

Settings shows two **Callback URLs**:

- **Delivery reports** — sent with every message automatically. Nothing to set up.
- **Replies (inbound SMS)** — give this to your gateway provider so replies reach your
  Inbox.

Under **Delivery reports** you can also **forward delivery reports to your own
endpoint**: reports are always kept in OMNI, and a copy is posted to your https address
as each one arrives.

## Send SMS

**SMS → Send SMS** sends straight from the channel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="To">
    Type numbers, pick contacts, or pick segments. Up to 500 numbers and contacts at a
    time; for more, upload a file in a [broadcast](/using/broadcasts). Picking a segment
    makes the send a broadcast, sent in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From">
    Pick one of your approved sender IDs. Where your provider doesn't require approved
    sender IDs, you can also leave it on your gateway login's default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message">
    **Use a template** and fill in its blanks, or **Write a message**. When your account
    sends approved templates only, the page says so and offers templates alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and send">
    Under the message, OMNI shows the encoding (GSM-7 or Unicode), the length, the number
    of segments, and how many messages that makes for everyone you picked. A preview
    shows the message as it will arrive. Confirm to send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**More options** holds three more choices:

- **Send later** — a date and time. Until then you can cancel it or send it early.
- **Keep trying for** — how long the carrier retries a phone that's off: the carrier's
  default, 1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day or 2 days.
- **High priority** — for codes and alerts that can't wait.

Once sent, OMNI opens the send's page under **Broadcasts**, where you can follow it.

<Frame caption="Send SMS: recipients, sender ID, template or free text, and a live preview.">
  <img src="/images/channels/sms-send.png" alt="The Send SMS page with the message form and a phone preview" />
</Frame>

You can also start an SMS from a contact's page with **Send SMS**.

<Tip>
  A message longer than one segment is sent as several and charged per segment. Unicode
  characters — many non-Latin scripts and emoji — make segments shorter.
</Tip>

## The SMS dashboard

**SMS → Dashboard** shows what the gateway reports live and what OMNI logged, over the
period you pick:

- your **gateway balance**, live;
- **Submitted** — messages and segments, with an estimated spend;
- **Delivery rate**, and how many failed;
- **Traffic** — submitted and failed over time;
- **What the carrier said** — the delivery report status, kept exactly as the gateway
  gave it;
- **Top failure reasons** and **Recent campaigns**;
- **Registration health** — your sender IDs and templates by status. Only approved ones
  are offered when you send.

## API & playground

People who can configure channels also see **API & playground**, when the plan includes
API access: the SMS gateway's own API with its documentation, a live runner and code
samples. For sending from your own software, most integrations use the
[OMNI API](/developers/overview) instead, which also manages
[sender IDs](/api-reference/sms/list-senders) and
[templates](/api-reference/sms/list-templates).

## Replies, opt-outs and limits

- Replies arrive in the [Inbox](/using/inbox) in the person's SMS thread.
- **STOP** stops a person on every channel; do-not-contact and quiet hours are always
  honoured. With the [Customer data](/batteries/customer-data) battery, people can stop
  SMS alone, or SMS marketing alone.
- When your provider enforces approval, a message from an unapproved sender ID, or one
  that matches no approved template, is refused. See
  [Sender IDs and DLT](/channels/sms-sender-ids-and-dlt).

## What batteries add on SMS

SMS works with no battery at all. Each one below adds to it when it is in your plan.

| Battery | What it adds on SMS |
| :--- | :--- |
| [AI Agents](/batteries/ai-agents) | An agent answers on SMS by filling in one of your approved templates word for word — only the blanks change. When no template fits, it drafts one and asks for it to be registered (after approval, if your policy says so). An inbound SMS can start or continue a workflow; questions go as "Reply 1 or 2". Reminder stories go by SMS when WhatsApp isn't available. You can ask about an SMS broadcast's results in plain words. |
| [Analytics](/batteries/analytics) | Reports on SMS traffic, delivery and failures — by error, sender and broadcast — and segments month by month; every account has it. |
| [Calendar](/batteries/calendar) | Appointment reminders and confirmations go by SMS when the person has no open WhatsApp route and the text matches a template. The one-time code for booking online can be sent by SMS. |
| [Catalogue](/batteries/catalogue) | Products are sent as names and prices with a link to your public catalogue page; `{{catalogue_link}}` in a broadcast leads to the same page. |
| [Customer data](/batteries/customer-data) | Someone can stop SMS marketing or SMS updates without stopping everything. You can require a recorded opt-in before SMS marketing, add your own stop and start words with an optional confirmation reply, show SMS on the preference page, use an audience as an SMS broadcast's recipients, and build audience rules on SMS consent. SMS messages feed last engaged, reply rate and preferred channel. |
| [Pipelines](/batteries/pipelines) | SMS messages show on a deal's timeline; a contact's deals show beside their SMS thread. |
| [Tickets](/batteries/tickets) | An SMS conversation can be raised as a ticket, and closing or assigning it keeps the ticket in step. |

---

Every RCS message is a **template**. You write it once in OMNI, then submit it to each
bot you send through; **each bot approves it separately**. A number can only be sent a
template the bot routed to its country has approved.

**RCS → Templates** lists your templates with their type, their variables and their
**Approval by bot**. Filter by **Waiting for approval**, **Approved somewhere** or
**Rejected**, or search by name.

<Note>
  With no bots yet, templates can be written but not submitted. Set up bots and routes in
  [RCS settings](/channels/rcs#how-you-connect) first.
</Note>

## Write a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a template">
    In **RCS → Templates**, choose **New template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name, type and category">
    The **name** uses letters, numbers and underscores. The **type** is fixed once
    saved. The **category** is Promotional, Transactional or OTP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the content">
    Fill in the parts the type asks for (below). Put a column of your recipient list in
    with `{{column}}` — for example `{{name}}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    Give **sample values for the preview** to see the message as a phone shows it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save template">
    Then submit it to your bots.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```text
Dear {{name}}, your EMI of {{amount}} is due on {{due_date}}.
```

### Types

| Type | What it is |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Text** | A message of up to 2,500 characters, with buttons. |
| **Rich card** | One card: an image or video (optional), a title, a description and up to 4 buttons. Choose the layout — **picture on top** or **picture beside** — and the media's height. |
| **Carousel** | 2 to 10 cards side by side, each with an image or video, a title, a description and up to 4 buttons. Choose the card width and the media's height. |
| **Text with image or video** | A message with one image or video, before or after the text. |
| **Text with PDF** | A message with a PDF, before or after the text. |

A card's title is up to 200 characters and its description up to 2,000. A message has
at most 11 buttons.

### Buttons

Each button has text of up to 25 characters and does one thing:

| Button | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Open a link** | Opens a web address. Variables work here too: `https://acme.in/pay/{{ref}}`. |
| **Call a number** | Dials the number you give. |
| **Quick reply** | Sends its text back as the person's answer, into your Inbox. |
| **Share location** | Asks the person to share where they are. |
| **Save to calendar** | Adds an event, with a start and end, to their calendar. |
| **Show on a map** | Opens a place, by latitude and longitude, with its name. |

Every tap is counted in the broadcast's **Button taps** and on the
[RCS dashboard](/channels/rcs#the-rcs-dashboard).

## Get it approved

Under **Approval by bot**, the template lists each of your bots with where it stands
there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tick the bots">
    Tick each bot you will send through. A bot that already has the template is ticked
    and locked; one that rejected it can be ticked again, with its reason beside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    Approval usually takes a few hours. If a bot's provider refuses the submission
    outright, OMNI says which bot and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check approval">
    While any bot is still deciding, **Check approval** asks the providers how each
    submission stands. Approvals and rejections also arrive on their own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the list, a template reads **Approved** when every bot has approved it, **Approved on
2 of 3** (for example) when some have, **Waiting for approval** while any is still
deciding, and **Rejected** otherwise.

**Already made it at the provider?** Link a template you made there: choose the **bot**,
enter **its template id there**, and OMNI asks the provider how it stands.

## Change or delete

A template can be changed only while no bot has it — or every bot turned it down. Once a
bot has it, the page says so and the content is locked; write a new template for new
wording.

**Delete** removes the template from OMNI. It stays at the provider.

## Send it

Approved templates are offered in **Send RCS** and in RCS
[broadcasts](/using/broadcasts), where each `{{variable}}` is filled from a column or
with your own text. See [Send RCS](/channels/rcs#send-rcs).

## Who may

Writing, submitting, linking and deleting RCS templates needs the permission to **manage
RCS templates** — the **Owner** and **Admin** roles by default.

A **channel** is one way of reaching people. OMNI has four:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SMS" icon="message" href="/channels/sms">
    Text messages from your sender IDs, with approved templates and India's DLT when you
    need it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WhatsApp" icon="whatsapp" href="/channels/whatsapp">
    Two-way conversations, approved templates, media, buttons and reactions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="RCS" icon="message-lines" href="/channels/rcs">
    Rich cards, carousels and buttons in the phone's own messages app, sent by bots routed
    by country.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Voice" icon="phone" href="/channels/voice">
    Numbers, IVRs, click-to-call, voice broadcasts and voice agents, with a call log and
    recordings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each channel is a separate part of the plan. A channel appears in the menu only when the
plan includes it; until it is connected, its section shows **Settings** alone, badged
**Set up**.

## Shared connection or your own

Every channel connects one of two ways, chosen on its Settings page under **Connection**
or **How you connect**:

| | The shared connection | Your own connection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SMS** | FireFlo's SMS gateway. Included, nothing to set up. | Your own gateway's address and login. |
| **WhatsApp** | A number FireFlo hosts for you — or, until yours is set up, FireFlo's shared number. No Meta setup. | Your number, in your own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) and Meta app. |
| **RCS** | Bots FireFlo runs and routes by country. | Your own login at your RCS provider, and your own bots. |
| **Voice** | FireFlo's Voice organisation, with numbers FireFlo gives you. | Your own Voice organisation, through its integration key. |

The shared connection is the quickest start. Your own connection gives you everything on
your provider account — and on Voice, it is required for
[voice broadcasts and voice agents](/channels/voice#what-needs-your-own-voice-organisation).
Switching back to the shared connection keeps your own details saved.

<Note>
  When FireFlo runs a channel for your account, the Settings page says so and there is
  nothing to connect. If a shared connection reads "Not offered on this platform yet",
  ask support@fireflo.au.
</Note>

The **Channels** page lists every channel with where it stands: **Not in plan**, **Not
configured**, **Configured · untested**, **Connected** or **Error**. A channel is active
when the plan includes it and a connection is configured — shared or your own.

## What every channel shares

The channels differ in what they carry, but the rest of OMNI treats them alike.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Inbox">
    Replies on SMS, WhatsApp and RCS land in one [Inbox](/using/inbox), in the person's
    thread for that channel. You answer on whichever channel you pick under **Reply
    via**. Calls appear on the contact's timeline instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Contacts and their timeline">
    Every message and call is filed against the [contact](/using/contacts), so their
    page shows one history across all four channels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Consent">
    Every channel honours **do-not-contact**, which stops everything, one-time codes
    included, and **quiet hours** — the person's own, or else the account's — during
    which promotional messages wait. When someone sends **STOP**, they stop hearing from
    you on every channel; **START** brings them back. With the [Customer data](/batteries/customer-data) battery, a
    person can instead stop one channel, or only its marketing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Broadcasts">
    A [broadcast](/using/broadcasts) can go out on any channel in the plan. People who
    opted out are left out before anything is sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The delivery report">
    What happened to each message — sent, delivered, read, failed and why — is on the
    [delivery report](/using/delivery-report), whatever the channel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The OMNI API">
    The [OMNI API](/developers/overview) sends on every messaging channel, with
    [failover](/developers/messages-with-failover) from one to the next, and
    [places calls](/developers/calls) on Voice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What each battery adds

A [battery](/batteries/overview) is an optional boost. Every channel works without any.
A battery adds the rows below only when it is in the account's plan.

| Battery | SMS | WhatsApp | RCS | Voice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI Agents** | Agents reply by filling in an approved DLT template; draft new templates; run workflows ("Reply 1 or 2"); answer questions about broadcasts. | Agents reply in free text inside the 24-hour window and by template outside it; draft and submit templates; run workflows with reply buttons; answer questions about broadcasts. | Answer questions about broadcasts. Agents don't reply on RCS, since it carries approved templates only. | — |
| **Calendar** | Reminders, confirmations and booking codes by SMS when there's no WhatsApp route and the text matches a DLT template. | Reminders with Confirm, Reschedule and Cancel buttons; a list of free times to book from; booking codes. | — | — |
| **Catalogue** | Products as names, prices and a link to your catalogue page. | Products pushed to your Meta catalogue; product cards and lists people can order from (on your own number); carts become orders. | Products as names, prices and a link to your catalogue page. | — |
| **Customer data** | Per-channel consent, opt-in for marketing, your own stop and start words, the preference page, audiences and traits. | The same. | The same. | Voice appears in consent and on the preference page, so someone can stop calls without stopping messages. |
| **Pipelines** | Messages on a deal's timeline; a contact's deals beside their thread. | The same. | The same. | — |
| **Tickets** | Raise a conversation as a ticket that stays in step with it. | The same. | The same. | — |

The channel pages say more about each row.

## Who may set up a channel

Connecting a channel and changing its settings needs the permission to **configure
channel modules**. By default the **Owner** and **Admin** roles have it. Sending needs
the permission to **send messages** (Owner, Admin and Support by default). See
[Team and roles](/using/team-and-roles) to change who holds what.

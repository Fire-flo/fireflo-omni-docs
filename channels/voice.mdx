**Voice** is FireFlo's voice service, run on FireTone. It answers your numbers with
IVRs, rings people from OMNI, keeps a log of every call with its recording, and — on
your own organisation — runs voice broadcasts and voice agents. Calls sit on each
contact's timeline beside their messages.

**The plan must include Voice.** The menu then shows a **Voice** section with Dashboard,
Calls, Voice agents, Numbers, IVRs and Settings.

## Shared organisation or your own

Open **Voice → Settings**. Under **Connection**, choose one of two:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="FireFlo's FireTone">
    Included; nothing to set up. You call through FireFlo's organisation, on the numbers
    FireFlo gives your account. FireFlo also designs your IVRs, sets each person's phone
    extension and connects your full call log — ask support@fireflo.au.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="My own FireTone">
    Your organisation's own numbers, phones, IVRs and voice agents, and its own bill.
    Enter, from FireTone:

    - the **Integration key** (FireTone → Developer → API keys → New key). It needs the
      click-to-call, read calls, campaigns and webhooks permissions, and OMNI's address
      among the key's allowed addresses, or FireTone refuses it.
    - the **Callback signing secret** (FireTone → Developer → Webhooks & callbacks). It is
      shown once, when it is made.
    - a **Caller ID** (optional) — one of your FireTone numbers, for calls to show as
      coming from.

    **Save and test** checks the key straight away.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Events from FireTone** shows whether FireTone has been heard from — how a call's
answer, end and recording reach OMNI. It is sent with every call automatically; there is
nothing to paste anywhere.

## What needs your own voice organisation

Most of Voice works the same on either connection. Two features need **your own
FireTone organisation**, because FireTone bills the organisation that places the calls:

- **Voice broadcasts** — ringing a whole audience into an IVR.
- **Voice agents** — an AI voice that calls a contact for you.

On FireFlo's organisation, Voice in a broadcast reads "Needs your own FireTone
organisation", and **Voice → Voice agents** explains how to connect it.

The rest differs only in who does what:

| | Your own FireTone | FireFlo's FireTone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Numbers** | Every number in your organisation, with its monthly rental | The numbers FireFlo gave your account; rental not shown |
| **Where a number's calls go** | One of your IVRs, an extension or another number | One of your IVRs; anything else is set by FireFlo |
| **The IVR designer** | Opened from OMNI | FireFlo designs IVRs for you |
| **Your phone (click-to-call)** | Each person picks their extension | FireFlo sets each person's extension |
| **The full call log** | An admin connects it in Voice → Calls | FireFlo connects it |
| **What calls cost** | Shown on the dashboard and on each call | Not shown |

## The Voice dashboard

**Voice → Dashboard** reports on the call log over 24 hours, 7 or 30 days: **Calls** (in
and out, with the change on the period before), **Answered** %, **Missed**, and **Talk
time** with the average length. On your own organisation it adds what FireTone charged.

A chart shows incoming, outgoing and missed calls per hour or day, and the breakdowns
show calls and answers **by kind**, **by your number**, **by IVR** and **by agent** (a
person or a voice agent).

## Who can be called

Voice is a channel people can stop, like any other.

- **Ringing someone into an IVR** and **calling with a voice agent** are screened for
  opt-outs, do-not-contact and quiet hours.
- **Voice broadcasts** leave out people who opted out or are marked do-not-contact, and
  ring only within the broadcast's calling hours.
- **Click-to-call**, where you talk to the person yourself, reaches anyone except a
  contact who asked never to be contacted. A STOP to messages doesn't stop you calling
  them.

## What batteries add on Voice

- [Analytics](/batteries/analytics) reports on calls: answered and missed, talk time, and
  calls by IVR, voice agent and hour of the day. Every account has it.
- With [Customer data](/batteries/customer-data), Voice appears in consent and on the
  preference page, so someone can stop calls without stopping messages.

Your sign-in and sign-up pages are the first thing a customer sees on your address.
**Sign-in pages** lets you design them: your headline and story around the form, your
channels and plans below it. The forms themselves do not change.

**Who can use it:** the **Owner** or **Reseller** role, on a reseller account. You need
[your own address](/using/branding) live for visitors to see the result.

Until you publish a design, your address shows the standard pages with your name, logo
and colour.

## Start from a layout

Open **Sign-in pages** and choose one of three layouts. Each one comes filled in with
your brand name and the channels you have switched on, so you can publish it as it is.

| Layout | What it looks like | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Side by side** | Your headline on the left, the form on the right, your sections down the page. | A page that should sell. |
| **Split screen** | A colour or image panel with your headline and points, the form beside it. | Customers who only come to sign in. |
| **Centred** | A band with your logo and headline, the form centred over it, sections below. | A strong picture, and phones. |

The layout applies to both pages. You can change it later and keep your text. On a
phone every layout shows the form first.

## Sections

Each page (**Sign-in page**, **Sign-up page**) has its own list of sections. Switch a
section on or off, move it up or down, and fill in its text. A section you switch off
keeps its text.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Headline and points** | Always shown, beside the form: a headline, a line under it, up to four points. |
| **Channels and features** | Up to six cards. With **Follow my channels** on, the cards are your switched-on channels and change by themselves. |
| **Plans and prices** | The sign-up plans you choose, with one marked *Most popular*. Names, prices and trial days come from the plan list and follow it. |
| **Figures** | Up to four figures with a label each. |
| **Customer logos** | Up to eight images. |
| **Customer quotes** | Up to three quotes with a name and role. |
| **Questions** | Up to eight questions and answers. |
| **Contact** | An email address, a phone number, working hours. |
| **Footer links** | Up to six links, such as terms and privacy. Links must start with `https://`. |

<Note>
The plans you choose under **Plans and prices** are also the only plans the sign-up form
on your address offers, in the same order. Choose none to offer every plan that is open
for sign-up.
</Note>

### Values that fill themselves

Type one of these words in any text and the page shows the current value. When the value
changes in the panel, the page follows without being published again.

| Type this | The page shows |
| --- | --- |
| `{brand}` | Your brand name. |
| `{trial_days}` | The trial length of your most popular plan. |
| `{channels}` | Your switched-on channels, such as *SMS, WhatsApp and RCS*. |
| `{plan_count}` | How many plans you offer at sign-up. |
| `{year}` | The current year. |

## Look and feel

Choose the panel colour (dark, your brand colour, or light), the headline font, the
corner style, an optional second accent colour, and a hero image with its darkening.
Images are PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB each, 16 for a design.

## Search and sharing

Set the page title (60 characters), the description (160 characters) and the image shown
when the link is shared. **Let search engines list these pages** is on by default;
switch it off to keep the pages out of search results.

## Draft, preview, publish

Changes are saved to a draft with **Save draft**. Visitors see nothing new until you
choose **Publish**.

- **Preview** opens the draft on your address in a new tab. Only someone with that link
  sees it, and the link changes each time you publish.
- **Restore previous** puts the design that was live before the last publish back in
  your draft.
- **Back to the standard pages** takes your design off your address. Your draft is kept.

Publishing is refused, with the reason, when a page has no headline or a section is
switched on with nothing in it.

## Tracking

You can paste a tracking code, such as an analytics tag. Because it runs on pages where
your customers type a password, it is handled more carefully than the rest of the
design:

- Only an **Owner** can see or change it, and the password is asked again.
- It **does not run until FireFlo has allowed it**. Any change to the code
  has to be allowed again.
- Every change is recorded with who made it and when, and emailed to the account's
  owners.
- It never runs in a preview, on any signed-in screen, or on another address.

The **Tracking** tab shows whether the code is *Not set*, *Waiting to be looked at* or
*Running*.

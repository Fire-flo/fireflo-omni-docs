The panel shows you only what your roles allow and your plan includes. Two people in
the same account can see different menus, and that is expected.

## The menu

The menu lists the screens you work in, in groups:

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | [Dashboard](/using/dashboard), [Inbox](/using/inbox), [Contacts](/using/contacts), [Broadcasts](/using/broadcasts), [Delivery report](/using/delivery-report), [Downloads](/using/downloads) |
| **Billing** | [Billing](/using/billing), Transactions, [Plan](/using/plan-and-modules), and [Resellers](/using/resellers) for a reseller |
| **Channels and batteries** | Each channel and battery your account has is **one entry**, such as SMS, WhatsApp or AI agents. Click it to see only its own screens, and **Back** to return. See [Channels](/channels/overview) and [Batteries](/batteries/overview) |
| **Developer Tools** | One entry, last in the menu, when your plan includes the API: API & playground, API keys, Webhooks, Routes, Templates, Messages and Requests. See [Developers](/developers/overview) |

A badge beside an item shows waiting work, such as unread conversations in the Inbox.
A folded group still shows the total on its header, so nothing waiting is hidden.

**Top menu or sidebar.** On a desktop the menu runs across the top by default. To have it
down the side instead, open your account menu (your initials, top right) and choose
**Use the sidebar**. In the sidebar, when you're on a channel's or battery's screen the
menu opens inside it, with **Back** to the rest; you can fold the other groups, and press <kbd>[</kbd> to shrink
it to icons and back. The panel remembers your choice.

**On a phone** the bar at the bottom holds Dashboard, Inbox and Contacts, with **Menu**
for everything else.

## The account icons

The icons at the top right are about the account rather than the day's work:

| Icon | What it opens | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Channels** | Each channel your plan includes and whether it is connected | Everyone in the account |
| **Branding** | Your brand and web address, for your customers | Resellers only |
| **Settings** | Business name, timezone, date format and quiet hours | Owner and Admin |
| **Team** | Members and their roles | Everyone who may see the team |

## Balance, notices and your account menu

- **Balance**: when your role may see it, your prepaid balance sits in the top bar.
  It turns red below zero. Select it to open [Billing](/using/billing).
- **Notices**: the bell holds short news for you, such as a new booking, each with a
  link to where it happened, and a count of unread ones.
- **Your account menu** (your initials) shows your account and roles, switches between
  the top menu and the sidebar, sets the **Appearance** (Light, Dark or System), and
  signs you out.

## Banners about your plan

A banner across the top tells you when your plan needs attention: how many trial days
are left, when your paid time ends, or that a payment is due. While a payment is due,
sending, campaigns and agents are paused until FireFlo records it. The banner links to
**How to pay** on the [Plan](/using/plan-and-modules) page.

## When something is missing

If a screen you expect isn't there, one of three things is true:

1. **Your role doesn't allow it.** Ask an Owner or Admin. See
   [Team and roles](/using/team-and-roles).
2. **Your plan doesn't include it.** See [Plan and modules](/using/plan-and-modules).
3. **A channel isn't connected yet.** Channel screens and the messaging parts of the
   dashboard appear once a channel is set up. See [Channels](/channels/overview).

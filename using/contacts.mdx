Contacts answers **"who do we talk to?"** It is one contact book, shared by every
channel: a person is one contact, whichever channel they use.

<Frame caption="Contacts: segments on the left, the chosen segment's contacts in the table.">
  <img src="/images/using/contacts.png" alt="The Contacts screen with the segment list, search, letter strip and contacts table" />
</Frame>

**Who can use it:** anyone who may see contacts (Owner, Admin, Support and Viewer).
Adding, editing, importing, tagging and deleting needs permission to manage contacts
(Owner, Admin and Support).

## Segments, fields and tags

- **Segments** are the groups down the left: *All contacts*, each segment, and the
  contacts in no segment. A contact is in one segment at a time. Make a new segment
  from the list; rename or remove one from the gear beside it.
- **Fields** are what you know about a person beyond their name and number: an email,
  a loan amount, a renewal date. **Shared fields** apply to every contact; a segment can
  also have **fields of its own**. Set them up from the gear beside the segment (or
  beside *All contacts*, for shared fields). The table's columns follow the segment's
  fields.
- **Tags** are labels you can put on any contact, in any segment.

Renaming a segment moves all its contacts to the new name; past campaigns keep the
name they were sent with. Removing a segment keeps its contacts, without a segment.

## Finding people

- **Search** by name or number.
- Pick a **tag**, or a **letter** from the strip to jump through names.
- **Advanced** search looks inside fields: a value, a range of numbers or a range of
  dates.
- The **column picker** chooses which columns show, per segment.

## Adding and changing contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add one">
    Choose **Add contact**, and enter a name, a phone number and, optionally, a segment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change many at once">
    Tick contacts in the table to **apply a tag**, **move them to a segment**, or delete
    them. Deleting can't be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Selecting a contact opens their [contact page](/using/contact-page).

## Importing a file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Choose **Import**, and drop a CSV or Excel file (also TSV or plain text), up to 25 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map columns">
    For each column, say what it is: the phone number (required, and only one), the name,
    an email or customer id to recognise people by, a field, or **+ New field**. Choose
    **Don't import** to skip a column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Options">
    Choose what happens when a number is already in your contacts:

    | Choice | What it does |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Add and update** (recommended) | Fills in values from the file. Blank cells never clear what you have. |
    | **Replace** | Mapped columns overwrite what is saved. Blank cells clear the value, and mapped tags replace existing tags. |
    | **Only add new** | Numbers already in your contacts are skipped untouched. |

    Optionally, put everyone in a segment and add tags to everyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    The import runs in the background; you can close the page. When it finishes it
    shows how many contacts were created, updated, skipped and refused. Find it again
    under **Past imports**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Importing never deletes contacts or opt-outs. Someone who replied STOP stays stopped,
  even if they are in the file.
</Note>

## Exporting

**Export** downloads the contacts on screen, with the filters and columns you are
looking at, as a CSV. For a very large export, the panel offers **Save to Downloads**
instead, so the file is written in the background and kept in
[Downloads](/using/downloads).

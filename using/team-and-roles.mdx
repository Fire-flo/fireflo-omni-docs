Team answers **"who can sign in, and what may each of them do?"** Open it from the
**Team** icon at the top of the panel.

<Frame caption="Team: everyone who can sign in, with their roles.">
  <img src="/images/using/team.png" alt="The Team screen listing members, their roles and invitation state, with the add-a-member form" />
</Frame>

**Who can use it:** anyone who may see the team (Owner, Admin, Billing, Support and
Reseller) sees the list. Adding and removing members, and changing roles, needs the
**Owner** or **Admin** role.

**Plan:** your plan may limit how many people can sign in. Custom roles need a plan
that includes them.

## Members

Each member shows their name, email and roles. *Invitation pending* means they haven't
set a password yet; you can send the invitation again. When your plan limits seats,
the heading shows how many are used.

### Add a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter who">
    Under **Add a member**, enter their work email and name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick roles">
    Tick one or more roles. You can only pick roles whose permissions you already hold,
    and only an Owner can give the Owner role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add">
    Choose **Add member**. They are emailed a link to set a password. Once every seat is
    used, adding is refused until you remove someone or move to a plan with more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Change roles or remove someone

Choose **Edit roles** on a member, tick the roles they should hold, and save. Roles you
can't give or take away are locked. To take someone off the account, remove them.

Nobody can change their own roles or remove themselves; another Owner or Admin does it.

## Roles

<Frame caption="Roles: FireFlo's shared roles, and your own.">
  <img src="/images/using/roles.png" alt="The Roles screen listing shared and custom roles with the permissions each carries" />
</Frame>

**Roles** (from the Team screen) lists every role your account can give:

- **FireFlo's roles**, the same for every account: Owner, Admin, Billing, Support,
  Viewer and Reseller. What each one may do is set out in
  [Plans and roles](/get-started/plans-and-roles).
- **Your own roles**, marked *Yours*, which only your account can see.

### Make a role of your own

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new role">
    Choose to add a role, and name it after the job, such as "Sales rep".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what it may do">
    Tick its permissions, grouped by area: workspace, messaging, contacts, billing,
    channels, downloads and each battery. Only what your account can do is offered;
    anything your account doesn't have is shown but can't be ticked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, then give it">
    Save the role, then give it to people from **Team** like any other.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Making and changing custom roles needs the **Owner** or **Admin** role. On a plan
without custom roles, the screen says your product doesn't include them.

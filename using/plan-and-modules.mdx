Two screens under **Billing** answer **"what have we got?"**: **Plan** for the
subscription, and **Modules** for the channels and batteries it gives you. Modules is
reached from the Plan page: **Your channels and batteries →**.

**Who can use them:** Owner, Admin and Billing. Switching to another plan needs the
**Owner** or **Admin** role.

## Plan

<Frame caption="Plan: your product, its status, what it includes and how to pay.">
  <img src="/images/using/plan.png" alt="The Plan screen with the product name, status, monthly price, included features and payment instructions" />
</Frame>

The panel calls your plan your **product**. The screen shows:

- its **name** and description, with a **status**: *Trial* (with the days left),
  *Active*, *Payment due*, or *Complimentary*;
- the **monthly price**;
- when the **trial ends** or until when you have **paid**;
- **payment**: offline, by bank transfer or cheque;
- **what's included**: each feature your account has, with its limit where there is
  one ("Team members · up to 25").

**How to pay** gives the payment instructions. Your paid time starts once FireFlo has
recorded the payment. While a payment is due, sending, campaigns and agents are paused.

### Switch to another plan

When more than one plan is offered to you, **Switch product** lists them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose the plan you want, and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What changes">
    What's included changes straight away. The new price applies from your next
    payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What each plan includes, and what it costs, is set by FireFlo. See
[Plans and roles](/get-started/plans-and-roles#plans).

## Modules

<Frame caption="Modules: the channels and batteries this account has.">
  <img src="/images/using/modules.png" alt="The Modules screen listing channels and batteries with their state" />
</Frame>

Modules lists, in two sections, what your account has:

- **Channels**: how your messages leave. A channel sends once it is in your plan
  **and** connected.
- **What else you have**: the batteries, the parts of the panel beyond messaging.

Each shows one of three states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **On** | Your account has it, and it works. |
| **Set up** | It is in your plan, but not connected yet. Connect it from [Channels](/channels/overview). |
| **Off** | It is not in your plan. |

Things your account can't have at all are left off the list.

Adding a module is a change of plan. Switch plan above, or write to
[support@fireflo.au](mailto:support@fireflo.au).

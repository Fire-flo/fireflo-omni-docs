Downloads answers **"where is that report?"** It holds the exports someone chose to
keep, and the campaign reports OMNI writes in the background.

<Frame caption="Downloads: saved reports, ready to fetch again.">
  <img src="/images/using/downloads.png" alt="The Downloads table listing saved reports with their kind, size and date" />
</Frame>

**Who can use it:** anyone who may read messages (Owner, Admin, Support and Viewer).
Everyone with access sees the account's saved reports.

## What ends up here

Most exports go straight to your browser and are not kept. A report lands in Downloads
when:

- someone chooses **Save to downloads** on the [Delivery report](/using/delivery-report);
- a [contacts](/using/contacts) export is too large to download at once, and is saved
  here instead;
- someone chooses **Prepare** under a campaign's **Reports**
  ([Broadcasts](/using/broadcasts)). A very large report is split into parts;
- someone exports an [Analytics](/batteries/analytics) report, or a
  [schedule](/batteries/analytics-schedules) sends one.

Reports are CSV files; Analytics can also save Excel files.

## Using it

Each row shows the report's name, kind, size and when it was saved. A report being
written says so, and the page refreshes itself until it is ready. Then:

- **Download** fetches it;
- delete removes it.

Search finds a saved report by name.

<Note>
  The newest 50 reports are kept per account. When a new one is saved, the oldest
  beyond that are removed. Download anything you need to keep.
</Note>

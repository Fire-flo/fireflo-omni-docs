The delivery report answers **"did it get there?"** It lists every message, on every
channel, whether it came from a broadcast, the Inbox, a channel's send screen or the API.

<Frame caption="The delivery report: every message, every channel.">
  <img src="/images/using/delivery-report.png" alt="The delivery report table with time, channel, recipient, message, status and DLR columns" />
</Frame>

**Who can use it:** anyone who may read messages (Owner, Admin, Support and Viewer).

## Reading it

Each row is one message:

| Column | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | When it was sent |
| **Channel** | The channel it went on |
| **To** / **From** | The recipient, and the sender it went from |
| **Message** | What was sent |
| **Campaign** | The broadcast it belonged to, if any |
| **Status** | *pending*, *sent*, *delivered*, *read* or *failed* |
| **DLR** | The delivery report the carrier sent back, as the carrier wrote it (for example `DELIVRD`) |
| **Kind** | What kind of message it was |
| **Sent by** | Who or what sent it |
| **Status updated** | When the status last changed |

Use the column picker to show or hide columns; the panel remembers your choice.

## Finding messages

- **Search** by recipient.
- Pick a **date range**, in your account's timezone.
- Filter by **status** and by **channel**.

The [dashboard](/using/dashboard)'s *Needs attention* list links straight to failed
messages.

## Exporting

- **Export CSV** downloads what you are looking at, with the same filters, straight to
  your browser.
- **Save to downloads** writes the same export in the background and keeps it in
  [Downloads](/using/downloads), to fetch later or share with a colleague who has
  access.

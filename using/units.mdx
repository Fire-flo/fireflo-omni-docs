An account below a reseller doesn't pay for its messages with money. It sends with
**units**, which its reseller gives it. This page explains units for everyone involved:
the reseller that gives them, a sub-reseller that passes them on, and the customer that
uses them.

## One unit is one message

A unit is **one message of one channel and product type**: an SMS part, a WhatsApp
message, an RCS message, a call minute. Units are kept separately for each channel and
product type, such as SMS Transactional, SMS Promotional or WhatsApp Marketing. Units of
one kind can't be used for another.

A unit works for any country the account is [allowed to send to](/using/billing#countries-you-can-send-to).

## Who holds what

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="A reseller">
    FireFlo gives a reseller money (its wallet and pockets) and a price list. The reseller
    sets no prices and no markup. It gives units to the customers directly below it.

    **Units you can still give**, per channel and product type, is worked out from the
    money that pays for that channel, less what the units already given out would cost,
    divided by the reseller's own price in its home country. Giving more than that is
    refused. Giving units moves no money: each message is charged to the reseller when it
    is sent.

    What the reseller charges its customers for units is between them.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A sub-reseller">
    A reseller can make one of its customers a **sub-reseller**, with customers of its
    own, up to ten levels below FireFlo. A sub-reseller holds a real stock of units,
    received from the reseller above it, and passes on only what it holds. It has no
    pockets and no prices.

    A sub-reseller that sends messages itself uses its own units.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A customer">
    A customer below a reseller sees its **Units** on [Billing](/using/billing#accounts-below-a-reseller):
    what it has left of each kind. It keeps a wallet for its plan and battery fees only. It
    has no message pockets and sees no prices.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## When a message is sent

- **It uses one unit** of its channel and product type from the account that sent it. A
  long SMS uses one unit per part, and a call one per started minute.
- **It is charged to the top reseller**, the one FireFlo gave money to, at that
  reseller's price for the recipient's country. The sender's wallet doesn't move, and
  nor does a sub-reseller's.
- **A message the channel refuses gives its unit back**, and the money where it was
  charged.

## When something runs out

- **No units of a kind:** that account's sends of that kind are refused, with the units
  held and needed. A broadcast pauses and carries on by itself when units arrive. Other
  kinds carry on.
- **The reseller's money runs out:** sends on that channel pause for every account below
  it, whatever units they hold. They carry on when money arrives.
- **The reseller's money no longer covers the units given out:** its Customers page warns
  it. Nothing is taken back.

## Prices never change units

Units are message counts. When FireFlo changes a reseller's price, every unit balance
below it stays as it is. Only what each later message costs the reseller changes.

## SMS below a reseller

Accounts below a reseller send SMS under the reseller's own account on FireFlo's SMS
gateway, so the gateway charges the reseller at the reseller's price.

An account may also send SMS straight to the gateway over its own SMPP login. That login is
**switched off** when the units of the kind it sends run out. SMS sent over the login is
counted from the gateway's records shortly after it is sent, so a few messages sent just
before the login is switched off can take the balance below zero; they are still charged
to the reseller. When new units arrive, a **new login** is made, with a new system ID. Its
password has to be reset before it can be used: ask your provider.

## Unit history

Every movement of units is listed, newest first, with the units moved and the balance
after:

| Line | What it is |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Received** | Units given to this account by the one above it. |
| **Transferred down** | Units this account gave to one below it. |
| **Taken back** | Unused units taken back by the account above. |
| **Used** | Units messages used, grouped like the money ledger. |
| **Returned** | Units given back for messages the channel refused. |

Resellers see it under **Unit history** on [Customers](/using/resellers), and for each
customer on its **Units** tab. A customer sees its own under **Billing → Unit history**.

The **Catalogue** battery holds the products you sell: prices, stock, pictures and sets.
You edit them in one place, and OMNI keeps your WhatsApp catalogue, your public catalogue
page and your AI agents in step. Orders from a WhatsApp cart land here as orders.

<Note>
Your plan must include **Catalogue**.
</Note>

Open **Catalogue** from the menu. It has five tabs: **Products**, **Sets**, **Orders**,
**In conversations** and **Settings**.

## Products

The **Products** tab lists every product with its price, stock and whether it is on
WhatsApp. Search by name, SKU or category, and filter by stock (**Running low**, **Out of
stock**) or by WhatsApp sync (**On WhatsApp**, **Waiting to sync**, **Sync failed**, **Not
on WhatsApp**). **Export CSV** downloads every product.

### Add or change a product

Choose **Add product**, or open a product to change it. Save with **Save and sync**.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What customers see. |
| **SKU** | Your product code. WhatsApp calls it the retailer ID; it can't change once the product is on WhatsApp. |
| **Category** | For finding and grouping products. |
| **Description** | Agents quote this. Say what a customer would ask: material, size, care. |
| **Price** / **Sale price** | Leave the sale price empty when it isn't on sale. |
| **Stock** | A number, or empty to not count it — then just **In stock** or out of stock. |
| **On sale** | Off takes the product off sale. |
| **Link on your website** | Optional. Without one, customers get the product's own page on your public catalogue. |
| **Picture** | Square, at least 500 × 500 px; JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB. WhatsApp shows it in product cards. Save the product first, then add a picture. |
| **Sets** | The sets the product belongs to. |

Beside the form you see the product as a WhatsApp card and its public page link, and
**Changes** — every edit, by people and agents. A change an agent made carries an
**Undo**.

**Take off sale** hides a product from customers and agents. It is kept for orders that
already include it.

## Sets

A **set** is a collection of products an agent or a broadcast can send together — "Diwali
edit", say. A set goes out as one WhatsApp product list, or as one link on SMS and RCS.

## Product messages on each channel

How a product reaches a customer depends on what their channel can do:

| Channel | What the customer gets |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp on your own number**, with your WhatsApp catalogue connected | Real product cards and product lists they can tap and order from. |
| **WhatsApp on a shared number** | The picture, the price and a link. |
| **SMS** and **RCS** | The names and prices, with a link to your public catalogue page. |

Every product message goes out like any other message on that channel, so consent and
WhatsApp's 24-hour window apply. In a broadcast, `{{catalogue_link}}` leads to your public
catalogue page.

When a customer writes to you from a product card on WhatsApp, the Inbox shows **Asked
about** with the product beside their message.

The **In conversations** tab shows how your own products look to a customer — on
WhatsApp, and on SMS and RCS. Nothing on that tab is sent.

## Orders

**Orders** lists every order, newest first. An order comes from:

- **WhatsApp cart** — a customer orders from your product cards;
- **AI agent** — an agent drafts an order from what the customer asked for, and a person
  approves it;
- **By hand**.

Each order keeps its own copy of every line, so a later price change never rewrites what
someone ordered.

<Frame caption="Orders">
  <img src="/images/batteries/orders.png" alt="The orders list with order numbers, customers, totals and status" />
</Frame>

Open an order to see its items, the customer's note, what happened, the customer and —
with [Pipelines](/batteries/pipelines) — its deal. An order is **New**, **Confirmed**,
**Declined**, **Fulfilled** or **Cancelled**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm or decline">
    **Confirm and reply** sends your confirmation message on the channel the order came in
    on. **Decline…** lets you write why — "Sorry — that weave is sold out." — and sends it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fulfil">
    When it has gone out, choose **Mark fulfilled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

### WhatsApp catalogue

Products are edited in OMNI and pushed to the Meta catalogue connected to your own
WhatsApp Business Account. Changes made by people or agents reach WhatsApp within a few
minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a catalogue on Meta">
    Create one in Meta Commerce Manager for your WhatsApp Business Account, if you don't
    have one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it">
    In **Catalogue → Settings → WhatsApp catalogue**, choose the catalogue and **Connect and
    send products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in what's already there">
    **Import from Commerce Manager** pulls the products you already have on WhatsApp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Disconnect** stops the sync. Your products stay in OMNI, and nothing on Meta is deleted.

### Public catalogue

The page SMS and RCS messages link to. It shows only products on sale, with no stock
counts. Set its **page name** and copy the **catalogue link**. You can also choose when the
page says "only N left".

### Orders

- **Open a deal for each order** — with Pipelines, each order also opens a deal, so it
  moves through a board like other business.
- **Reply when an order is confirmed** — your message, with `{name}`, `{order}` and
  `{total}` filled in.

### What agents may do

With [AI Agents](/batteries/ai-agents), this is the default for new agents; each agent's
page can change its own.

| Action | Default |
|---|---|
| Find products — search the catalogue to answer a question | Automatic |
| Send products — a product card, a list, or a link on SMS and RCS | Automatic |
| Add a product — from a message or a photo someone sends | Needs approval |
| Change a product — name, description, price, sale price, stock | Needs approval |
| Take a product off sale | Needs approval |
| Draft an order — from what a customer asked for in the chat | Needs approval |
| Confirm or decline an order | Needs approval |

Agents quote prices and stock exactly as your catalogue gives them, and never pitch a
product that isn't listed. Workflows gain a **Send products** block.

## Who may use Catalogue

| Permission | Lets someone | Roles that have it by default |
|---|---|---|
| See products and orders | Open products, sets, orders and In conversations | Owner, Admin, Support, Viewer |
| Add and change products, sets and settings | Edit products and sets, and change catalogue settings | Owner, Admin |
| Confirm, decline and fulfil orders | Act on orders | Owner, Admin, Support |

An Owner or Admin can change these on the **Roles** screen. See
[Team and roles](/using/team-and-roles).

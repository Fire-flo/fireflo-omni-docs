Analytics puts OMNI's figures in one place: messages, conversations, contacts, broadcasts
and spend, and calls, tickets, deals, AI agents and the OMNI API when your account has
them. Open a **built-in report**, or **build your own** by choosing what to count and how
to group it — no formulas, no queries. Any report can be exported, shared with your team,
or [sent on a schedule](/batteries/analytics-schedules).

**Analytics needs nothing in your plan:** every account has it. Its **Analytics** entry
appears in the menu once a channel is on, and opens onto **Reports**, **New report** and
**Schedules**.

## The report library

**Analytics → Reports** lists:

- **Your team's reports** — the ones saved and shared with everyone, and your own. Pinned
  reports come first.
- **The built-in reports**, by category.

Search finds a report by its name, description or category. A category appears only when
your account has what it reports on, and you may see that data elsewhere in the panel —
**Spend** needs permission to see transactions, **Calls** permission to see the call log,
and so on.

| Category | Built-in reports |
| :--- | :--- |
| Messaging | Traffic by channel · Delivery and read rate by channel · Failures by error · Segments by channel, per month · Inbound and outbound · Top senders |
| Conversations | New conversations · Response times by team member · Busiest hours |
| Contacts | Contact growth · Opt-outs by how they asked |
| Broadcasts | Broadcast comparison · Broadcasts per month by channel |
| Spend | Spend per month · Top-ups and usage |
| Calls (with Voice) | Calls answered and missed · Calls by IVR · Voice agent calls · Busiest calling hours |
| Tickets (with [Tickets](/batteries/tickets)) | Tickets opened and solved · Ticket times by team member · Tickets by category · Solved by AI and by people |
| Pipeline (with [Pipelines](/batteries/pipelines)) | Deals won and lost by month · Open deals by stage · Deals from broadcasts |
| AI agents (with [AI Agents](/batteries/ai-agents)) | AI tokens by agent · AI usage by model |
| OMNI API (with the [OMNI API](/developers/overview)) | Where API messages got through · Why failover steps were refused · API requests and errors |

## Reading a report

- **Period.** Choose Today, Yesterday, the last 7, 30 or 90 days, this or last week, this
  or last month, this year, the last 12 months — or **Custom dates**, up to 800 days.
  Days are your account's, in its time zone: a message sent at 1:30 am counts on that
  day, wherever the server is.
- **Compare with the previous period** adds the period just before, of the same length.
  Each total then shows its change — a rate's change in percentage points ("−1.2 pts").
- **Totals**, one per measure, for the whole period.
- **The chart** — line, bar, stacked bars, area or pie. Switch it with the chips above
  it; the switch doesn't change the saved report.
- **The table**, with a totals row. A report grouped by something other than time shows
  its top rows (50 unless the report says otherwise) and adds up the rest as **Other**.
  At most 10,000 rows are counted; past that, the report says so — narrow the period or
  add a filter.

Built-in reports can't be changed: **Edit a copy** opens the copy in the designer. On
your own reports, and on shared ones if you may build reports, **Edit**, **Pin** and
**Delete** are there too.

### Exporting

**CSV** and **Excel** save the report, for the period on screen, to
[Downloads](/using/downloads). Figures are written as people read them: rates as
percentages, money in whole units of your currency, durations in seconds. The Excel file
has the report's name and period at the top and a bold totals row.

## Building a report

**Analytics → New report** builds one in seven steps. The preview on the right redraws as
you choose, with your real figures.

<Frame caption="New report: the steps on the left, the live preview on the right.">
  <img src="/images/batteries/analytics-new.png" alt="The report designer" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Data">
    What the report is about. Each source counts one kind of thing — see the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measures">
    What to count, up to six: messages, delivered, delivery rate, average first response…
  </Step>
  <Step title="Group by">
    Up to two things: a time — day, week, month, hour of day or day of week (one time at
    most) — and anything the source offers, such as channel, status or team member. With
    nothing chosen, the report is a single figure per measure. Grouped by something other
    than time, choose how many rows to show; the rest are added up as Other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filters">
    Up to ten: keep or leave out values of a field. The values offered are the ones your
    data holds, the most common first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Period">
    The period the report opens on, and whether it compares with the period before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show as">
    Line, bar, stacked, area, pie, a single number, or a table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and sharing">
    A name, an optional description, and who sees it: **Everyone in the account**, or
    **Only me**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What each source counts

| Source | Measures | Group and filter by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Messages** | Messages, sent, delivered, read, failed, segments, delivery rate, read rate, failure rate | Channel, direction, status, error, sender, broadcast, kind, sent by |
| **Conversations** | Conversations, open, closed, answered, average first response, average time to close, answered share | State, assigned to, priority |
| **Contacts** | Contacts added, opted out since, do not contact | Segment |
| **Opt-outs** | Opt-outs, by when they asked | How they opted out, segment |
| **Broadcasts** | Broadcasts, recipients, delivered, failed, left out (opted out), delivery rate | Broadcast, channel, status |
| **Spend** | Spent, topped up, entries | Kind, description (group only) |
| **Calls** | Calls, answered, not answered or missed, talk time, average call, answer rate | Kind, direction, status, IVR, voice agent, placed by, your number, how it ended |
| **Tickets** | Tickets, solved, still open, solved by AI, average first response, average time to solve, solved share, solved by AI of those solved | Status, priority, category, assigned to, how it started, handled by, solved by |
| **Deals** | Deals, won, lost, closed, value, value won, win rate, average time to close | Pipeline, stage, status, from broadcast |
| **AI usage** | Things done, model calls, tokens in, tokens out, average model time | Agent, model, AI provider, what happened |
| **Messages with failover** | Messages, succeeded, failed, success rate, average time to finish | Status, got there on, how far it got, why it failed, template, purpose |
| **Failover steps** | Steps tried, got there, refused, timed out, got there of those tried | Channel, step, ended, why it was refused |
| **API requests** | Requests, errors, error rate, average time | Method, answer, error, API key, from (your systems or the playground) |

A few things to know:

- **Delivery rate** is delivered (or read, or replied to) out of those sent; **read rate**
  is read out of those delivered.
- **First response** is the time from a conversation's start to the first answer from
  your team or an AI agent.
- **Money** is shown in your account's currency. Spend comes from your balance's
  transactions, so it has no per-channel split. Calls and AI usage report time and
  tokens, not money.
- **Message figures for past days** are counted once a day and settle after two days, as
  late delivery reports arrive; today's are counted live.

## Who may

| To | Permission | Roles by default |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| See reports, change their period, compare | See analytics reports | Owner, Admin, Support, Viewer |
| Build, change, share and export reports | Build, change, share and export analytics reports | Owner, Admin, Support |
| Schedule reports and choose where they go | Schedule reports and choose where they are sent | Owner, Admin |

On top of these, each source needs the permission that shows that data elsewhere — the
message log for Messages, transactions for Spend, tickets for Tickets — so a report never
shows someone what the rest of the panel would hide.

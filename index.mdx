---

FireFlo OMNI is where a business talks to its customers. Every message, on every
channel, lands in **one Inbox**. Every person is **one contact**, whichever channel
they wrote on. And everything the panel does, your own systems can do through **one
API**.

</Frame>

## What OMNI is made of

OMNI is built from modules. Your plan decides which ones your account has.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Channels" icon="tower-broadcast" href="/channels/overview">
    How messages leave and arrive: **SMS**, **WhatsApp**, **RCS** and **Voice**. Use
    FireFlo's connection, or connect your own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Batteries" icon="battery-full" href="/batteries/overview">
    What else the panel can do: **AI Agents**, **Calendar**, **Catalogue**,
    **Customer data**, **Pipelines** and **Tickets**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="OMNI API" icon="code" href="/developers/overview">
    One API for every channel: send with failover between channels, manage contacts,
    answer conversations and receive signed webhooks.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Whatever modules you have, a few things are always there: the **Inbox**,
**Contacts**, **Broadcasts**, the **Delivery report**, **Downloads**, **Billing** and
your **Team**.

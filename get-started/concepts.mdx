A few ideas explain almost everything in OMNI. Read this page once and the rest of
the panel will make sense.

## Accounts

An **account** is one business. Everything belongs to an account: its contacts,
conversations, broadcasts, balance, team and settings. Nothing is shared between
accounts.

People sign in as **members** of an account. Each member holds one or more
**roles**, and the roles decide what they may see and do. See
[Plans and roles](/get-started/plans-and-roles).

Each account has its own business name, timezone and date format. The timezone is
used for scheduled broadcasts and for dates written into messages.

## Resellers and their customers

Some accounts are **resellers**. A reseller sets up accounts for businesses of its
own, its **customers**. It puts each one on a plan, funds them with credit from its
own balance, and can add a markup on top of its prices.

- A reseller's customer is an ordinary account. It runs its own team and sees the
  panel under the reseller's brand: name, logo, colour and, once set up, its own
  web address.
- A customer can never do more than its reseller can. Its roles are limited to what
  the reseller's account may do.
- A reseller's customer can't have customers of its own.
- FireFlo turns an account into a reseller. It isn't something an account can
  switch on for itself.

See [Resellers](/using/resellers) and [Branding](/using/branding).

## Plans and features

A **plan** is what an account has bought. It is made of **features**. Some features
are switched on or off (custom roles, API access, reseller accounts, each channel).
Others are limits (how many team members may sign in).

FireFlo decides what each plan includes and what it costs. FireFlo can also change
one feature for one account without changing its plan. The panel always shows the
result: what *your* account has today.

A plan has a monthly price and, often, a trial. Payment is offline (bank transfer or
cheque). Your paid time starts once FireFlo records the payment. If a payment falls
due, sending, campaigns and agents pause until it is recorded.

See [Plan and modules](/using/plan-and-modules).

## Roles and permissions

A **role** is a set of permissions, such as "send messages", "manage contacts" or
"see the balance". OMNI starts every account with six shared roles: **Owner**,
**Admin**, **Billing**, **Support**, **Viewer** and **Reseller**. On a plan that
includes them, an account can also make **custom roles** of its own.

Permissions decide what you can do. The plan decides what the account has. A screen
appears only when both allow it. That is why two people in the same account can see
different menus.

## Connections: FireFlo's or your own

Each channel needs a connection to the networks that carry its messages. For most
channels there are two choices:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="FireFlo's connection">
    FireFlo provides the connection. Nothing to set up: for SMS it is included, and
    for WhatsApp FireFlo hosts a number for you, with no Meta setup. Messages are
    priced from your account's price list and shown under
    [Billing](/using/billing).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Your own connection">
    You bring your own: your own SMS gateway's address and login, or your own
    WhatsApp Business Account in your own Meta Business account. Credentials are
    encrypted when saved and never shown again. You can switch back to FireFlo's
    connection later without losing your saved details.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Which choices you have depends on what FireFlo offers on your platform and, for a
reseller's customer, on whether the reseller allows its own connection. Each
channel's page explains its options: see [Channels](/channels/overview).

## People and consent

A **contact** is one person, identified by their phone number. They are the same
contact on every channel, with one thread in the Inbox.

OMNI checks every message against what the person has said before it sends:

| Rule | What it stops |
| --- | --- |
| **Opt-out** | A person who replies **STOP** (or UNSUBSCRIBE, OPTOUT, CANCEL, END, QUIT) on any channel stops messages they didn't ask for. **START** (or UNSTOP, SUBSCRIBE, OPTIN) undoes it. One-time codes still go. |
| **Do not contact** | A hard no, set by your team on the contact's page. Nothing reaches the person: not a campaign, not a reply, not a one-time code. It can last forever or lift on a date. |
| **Quiet hours** | A person's own quiet hours, in their own timezone, hold marketing messages. Messages they asked for still go. |

An opt-out is kept even if the contact is deleted and later added again, and an
import never removes one.

Each broadcast says whether it is **marketing** or **an update the person
expects**. Marketing skips people who stopped offers; an update skips only people
who stopped everything.

The [Customer data](/batteries/customer-data) battery adds finer control on top:
stops per channel and per purpose, opt-in for marketing, and a preference page.

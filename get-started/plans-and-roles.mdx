Two things decide what someone sees in OMNI: the **roles** they hold, and the
**plan** their account is on. A role says what a person may do. A plan says what the
account has. A screen appears only when both allow it.

## The six shared roles

Every account starts with six roles. They work the same in every account. A member
can hold more than one, and gets everything each of them allows.

| Role | Who it is for | What it may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The person responsible for the account | Everything the account can do. On a reseller account, this includes managing customers and allocating credit to them. |
| **Admin** | People who run the account day to day | Everything an Owner can do, except managing a reseller's customers and allocating credit. |
| **Billing** | Finance | See the balance, the transactions and the plan, and who is on the team. No messages or contacts. |
| **Support** | Agents who talk to customers | Read and reply in the Inbox, send messages and broadcasts, manage contacts, and label conversations. Day-to-day work in the batteries. No settings, no billing. |
| **Viewer** | People who only need to look | Read the Inbox, contacts, the Delivery report and Downloads, and view what the batteries hold. Can't send or change anything. |
| **Reseller** | People who look after a reseller's customers | See the balance and transactions, manage customers and allocate credit to them, and see the team. |

### Rules for giving roles

- **You can only give a role whose permissions you already hold.** An Admin can't
  make someone an Owner and pick up an Owner's powers that way.
- **Only an Owner can give the Owner role.**
- **Nobody changes their own roles, or removes themselves.** Another admin does.
- **Team size follows the plan.** When the plan limits team members, the panel shows
  how many seats are used, and refuses a new member once they are all taken.

## Custom roles

On a plan that includes custom roles, an account can make roles of its own, such as
"Sales rep", from the same permissions the shared roles use. They appear beside the
shared roles, marked as yours, and only your account can see or give them.

A custom role can only carry what your account may do. On a reseller's customer
account, that also means nothing the reseller itself can't do.

See [Team and roles](/using/team-and-roles).

## Plans

A plan is a set of **features**. A feature is either switched on or off (custom
roles, single sign-on, the audit log, reseller accounts, API access, each channel,
each battery) or a **limit** (how many team members may sign in).

**The operator decides what each plan includes, what it costs and what it is called.**
As an example of how plans are often built:

| Example plan | Who it suits | Features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | Small teams getting set up | Up to 5 team members, API access |
| **Growth** | Growing teams | Up to 25 team members, custom roles, API access, audit log |
| **Enterprise** | Large organisations and resellers | Unlimited team members, custom roles, API access, audit log, single sign-on, reseller accounts |

Your own plan may look nothing like this. The panel always shows what yours includes,
under **Plan**. FireFlo can also change a single feature for your account without
changing your plan.

See [Plan and modules](/using/plan-and-modules).

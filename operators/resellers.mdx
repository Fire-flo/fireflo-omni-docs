A **reseller** is one of your customers that sells OMNI on to customers of its own. Its
customers are ordinary OMNI accounts that sit **under** the reseller. With a
[domain of its own](/operators/domains-and-email#domains), it can present the panel under
its own brand.

This page is the operator's view. For what a reseller sees, see
[Resellers](/using/resellers); for how units work, see [Units](/using/units).

## How a reseller is billed

- **You give the reseller money and a price list.** Its wallet and pockets, topped up
  from its page like any customer's, and the [price list](/operators/pricing) it pays.
  Those are the only prices in its world.
- **The reseller gives its customers units.** One unit is one message of one channel and
  product type. Staff never give units.
- **Each message is charged to the reseller.** A customer's message uses one of its units
  and is charged to the reseller's money at the reseller's price for that country.
- **Resellers set no prices and no markup.** Its customers have no prices and no message
  pockets. Each keeps a wallet for its plan and battery fees only, which the reseller
  funds with credit.

## Make an account a reseller

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account">
    Find it under [Platform → Customers](/operators/customers) and open its page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn reselling on">
    On **Overview**, select **Edit**, tick **They resell to their own customers** and
    select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it money and prices">
    Top up its wallet, or the pocket of each channel it resells, from **Billing**. Choose
    the list it pays on its **Reseller** tab. For SMS on your gateway, use the gateway's
    currency.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The account now shows a **Reseller** badge. Its people with the **Owner** or
**Reseller** role get a **Customers** page in their menu.

You can't turn reselling off while the account still has customers of its own.

### Sub-resellers

A reseller can make one of its own customers a **sub-reseller**, with customers of its
own. A tree can go up to ten levels below you. A sub-reseller holds the units it received
and passes on only those. Every message anywhere in the tree is charged to the top
reseller, the one you gave money to.

## The Reseller tab

A top-level reseller's page has a **Reseller** tab:

- **Price list the reseller pays**. A change waits to apply on the SMS gateway like any
  other price change.
- **Pockets that pay for its customers**, with its wallet. Money held at the SMS gateway
  is marked so.
- **On the SMS gateway**: its customers' SMS logins sit under its own gateway account, so
  the gateway charges the reseller's balance at the reseller's price.
- **Its customers · units and what they cost the reseller**: every account in its tree,
  with units left and used, what their messages have cost the reseller, their SMS logins,
  and a status such as **No units given**, **SMS units low**, **No units** or **No units ·
  login off**. **Export** downloads the table.

A warning shows when the reseller's money has run out on a channel, which pauses sends for
its whole tree, or when its money no longer covers the units outstanding below it at its
current prices. Units are read-only here.

## When something runs out

- **A customer has no units** of a kind: its sends of that kind are refused, and a broadcast
  pauses until units arrive.
- **The reseller's money runs out** on a channel: sends on that channel pause for every
  account below it, whatever units they hold, and carry on when money arrives.
- **A price changes**: units never change. Only what each later message costs the
  reseller does.

## SMS below a reseller

Accounts below a reseller send SMS under the reseller's own account on your SMS gateway.
The gateway charges the reseller, at the reseller's price; OMNI counts the messages
against each sender's units.

When an account below a reseller sends straight to the gateway over its own SMPP login,
OMNI switches that login off once the units of the kind it sends run out. The gateway has
no way to switch a login back on, so when new units arrive OMNI makes a new login, with a
new system ID. Reset its password under the customer's **Modules** tab and pass it on.

## See a reseller's customers

- On the reseller's page, the **Customers** tab lists the accounts under it.
- On Platform → Customers, the **Under** column names each account's reseller, and
  **Any reseller** narrows the list to one reseller's customers.
- The **Resellers** chip lists every reseller.

A reseller's customer is a full account on its own page. Its **Billing** tab shows its
units, its plan and its fee wallet; it has no prices. You can change it, top up its fee
wallet or suspend it like any other.

## What a reseller does for its customers

From its **Customers** page, a reseller can:

- **Set up a customer**, with its product, trial and an opening credit for its fee
  wallet. The owner is invited by email.
- **Put a customer on a product.** Resellers can choose any of your active products,
  including hidden ones, at your price. When a reseller moves a customer to a dearer
  product, time already paid for is worth less of it, so the paid-until date moves in to
  match.
- **Give units** and **take back** unused ones.
- **Add credit** to the customer's fee wallet, from the reseller's own balance.
- **Narrow where it sends**, choosing allowed countries from the reseller's own.
- **Decide about SMS** (see below).
- **Suspend** a customer, and let them back in.

The customer runs its own team. The reseller can see who can sign in, but can't change
them.

### Their own SMS gateway

When it sets a customer up, and later on that customer's page, a reseller decides whether
the customer **May connect their own SMS gateway**. Turned off, the customer stays on the
SMS connection the reseller provides. Where the reseller's SMS runs on your connection,
the choice isn't offered: the customer gets a login under the reseller's gateway account.
Other channels aren't affected.

### Suspension

A reseller can suspend one of its customers. Nobody there can sign in until the reseller
lets them back in, and anything still running stops. Their data, settings and credit are
kept. This is the reseller's own switch. It is separate from **Suspend** on the
customer's page under Platform → Customers, which only staff use.

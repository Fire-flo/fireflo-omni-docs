OMNI is built from **modules**:

- **Channels** carry messages and calls: SMS, WhatsApp, RCS and Voice.
- **Batteries** add what the panel can do beyond messaging: AI Agents, Calendar,
  Catalogue, Customer data, Pipelines and Tickets.
- **Pluggable modules** sit in front of everything else. The **OMNI API** is one. They
  are optional: without them, every channel and battery works as before.

**Platform → Modules** shows which of these your deployment carries, at what version, and
how many accounts use each. It is the page to open when something seems to be missing.

## How a module reaches a customer

Three things must all be true:

1. **The deployment carries it.** A module this deployment doesn't load has no pages and
   no menu entries for anyone, whatever their plan says.
2. **It isn't switched off for everyone** on this page (or, for a channel, under
   [Channels](/operators/channel-connections#switching-a-channel-off-for-everyone)).
3. **The customer's product includes it**, or you've given it to them with an
   [override](/operators/customers#modules). A channel also needs a connection, shared or
   their own.

A module with no plan feature is on for everyone it reaches.

## Turning a module on for customers

This page doesn't decide who has a module. That is set in two places:

- **For a product**: [Products](/operators/products-and-plans). Every customer on it gets
  the module.
- **For one customer**: the **Modules** tab on [their page](/operators/customers#modules).

## Reading the page

The figures at the top show the core and panel versions, how many modules are loaded out
of those installed, and **Health**: **Clear**, or the number of issues to look at.

Each issue is spelled out above the list:

| Issue | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Is two different versions** | The two halves of the module (the server and the panel) are at different versions. A page can call something that isn't there yet. |
| **Is on disk but not loaded** | The deployment carries the module but doesn't load it. Its pages and menu entries don't appear for anyone. |
| **Is missing something it needs** | It depends on another module that isn't loaded at a version it accepts. |
| **Is switched on and nobody has it** | No account's plan includes it. Not a fault, but worth knowing. |

Each module's row shows its badges (**Not loaded**, **Version mismatch**, **Off for
everyone**), what it depends on, its version, and how many accounts use it. For a
channel, that's accounts that have it configured. For anything else, it's accounts whose
plan includes it.

## Switching a module off for everyone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the module">
    Select **Manage** on its row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch it off">
    Under **For every account**, select **Switch off for everyone**. The page tells you
    how many accounts have it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Switching off is immediate and reversible. The module disappears from every account's
menus and pages, and nothing is deleted. Select **Switch back on** to restore it. For a
channel, this is the same switch as under
[Channels](/operators/channel-connections#switching-a-channel-off-for-everyone), and its
open broadcasts are cancelled.

<Note>
  Switching off stops a module reaching anyone, but the deployment still loads it. To
  stop carrying a module altogether, contact support@fireflo.au.
</Note>

## Backing up and deleting a module's data

The **Its data** section of **Manage** shows how many rows the module holds, and in how
many tables.

**Back up** downloads all of it to you as one file. The page checks that what you
received matches what was sent, and says so. **Nothing is kept on the server.** The file
you download is the only copy.

Deleting a module's data is permanent. The page refuses until every reason not to has
been dealt with, and lists them all:

- the module is still switched on;
- some accounts still have it;
- another module still depends on its data, and has to be emptied first;
- no backup has been taken, the last one was never confirmed as received in full, or the
  last one has already been used for a delete.

When nothing stands in the way, type the module's key to confirm and select **Delete
this data**.

<Warning>
  This empties every table the module holds and can't be undone. Keep your backup
  somewhere you trust before you delete.
</Warning>
